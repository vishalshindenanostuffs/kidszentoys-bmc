Our Story

Born from a Parent's Purpose

KidsZen Toys was founded in 2022 by Sanyogita Bamb, an IT engineer who set aside her corporate career to follow a deeper calling. After witnessing the transformative impact that thoughtful play has on young minds, she earned a master's degree in child psychology and channelled that knowledge into building a toy brand rooted in real developmental science.

Based in Pune, India, KidsZen Toys (Kidszen Toys LLP) designs activity-based games and kits for children from birth through ten years old. Every product is built to reduce screen time, sharpen fine motor skills, grow emotional awareness and bring families together around a table rather than a device.

What started with the Travel Activity Kit has grown into a range of over a dozen purposeful products — from Velcro sorting kits for toddlers to cultural board games for older children — each one the product of deep thought about what children actually need.
